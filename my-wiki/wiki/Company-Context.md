# Company Context & Core Objectives

* **Company Name:** ApexRetail AI
* **Industry/Niche:** B2B SaaS - Supply Chain & Inventory Optimization
* **Target Audience:** Mid-to-large market e-commerce brands doing $5M–$50M in annual revenue. (See target competitor vulnerabilities in [[Market-And-Risks]]).
* **Core Value Proposition:** We use predictive modeling to prevent stockouts and overstocking, saving retail brands an average of 18% in capital lockup.
* **Current Business Goals:**
  1. Reduce customer onboarding churn by making data integration instant rather than taking 2 weeks.
  2. Scale the platform to support real-time inventory updates for 500+ active enterprise clients simultaneously (Directly addressed by [[Project-Specification]]).
  3. Lower our monthly cloud processing costs which are currently being drained by our legacy infrastructure detailed in [[Technical-Architecture]].
